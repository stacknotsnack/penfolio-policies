Penfolio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

Penfolio is a personal diary app for tracking medication, symptoms, weight and food. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information, in plain language.

The short version

Everything you enter into Penfolio — your medication, doses, symptoms, weight, measurements, food entries and notes — is stored only on your own device. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server that Penfolio sends this information to. We (the developer) never see it.

What is stored, and where

All entries are saved in a local database and local preferences on your device only. Nothing is backed up to any server we control. If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted with it. You can also erase everything at any time from Settings → "Delete all my data".

Delete your data

Everything Penfolio stores lives only on your device, so deleting it is entirely in your control:

  • Delete specific entries at any time from within the app — for example, swipe to delete a food entry, or remove a logged dose or measurement.
  • Delete everything at once from Settings → "Delete all my data". This immediately and permanently removes every entry, on this device, with no confirmation email or waiting period.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all of its data along with it, since nothing is kept on any server.

There is no account to close and no separate data-deletion request process, because Penfolio has no copy of your data to delete on our end — the copy on your device is the only copy that ever exists.

The one feature that uses the internet

Scanning a food barcode sends only the scanned barcode number to Open Food Facts, a free, independent, open food database, so it can look up that product's nutrition information. No other information — not your name, your other entries, your symptoms, or your medication — is sent with that request. Typing a food item in by hand never uses the internet at all.

Camera

Penfolio asks for camera access only to let you scan a barcode. The camera is used live, on-device, to read the barcode; no photo or video is saved, stored, or sent anywhere.

Notifications

Reminders (for injections, check-ins, etc.) are scheduled locally on your device using Android's own notification system. No push notification service or external server is involved.

Exporting your data

Penfolio lets you export your own data as CSV and JSON files via Settings → "Export CSV and JSON". This uses Android's share sheet so that you can choose where the files go — for example, saving them or sending them to your clinician. Penfolio does not send this export anywhere on its own.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in of any kind
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No selling or sharing of your data with third parties
  • No cloud backup of your entries

Children

Penfolio is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change, and — for any material change — the app's onboarding or Settings screen will note it.
